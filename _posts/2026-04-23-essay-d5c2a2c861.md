My idea of Humanistic Capitalism and Human Sustainability
我对人文资本主义和人类可持续性的理解
Speech by Brunello Cucinelli to the World's Great Leaders on the occasion of the G20
布鲁内洛·库西尼利在 G20 峰会上的讲话

Rome, 31 October 2021  罗马，2021 年 10 月 31 日

I am honoured to be here among you, the distinguished leaders of our wonderful Earth. I will try with some emotion to express to you my idea of Human Sustainability and what I mean by Humanistic Capitalism.
我很荣幸能来到这里，与各位杰出的地球领导者们相聚。我将怀着一些激动的心情，向你们表达我对人文可持续发展的理念，以及我所说的“人文资本主义”的含义。

I spent the first part of my life in the country, we were farmers, we did not have electricity, we worked the land with animals, we collected rainwater, we had a great respect for the earth. The Greek philosopher and poet Xenophanes stated: 'Everything comes from the earth", and we lived in harmony with Creation. The first bale of grain we harvested went to the community at my grandfather's behest. From there I learned the great theme of life: the balance between profit and giving back; that period of my life is still a gift to my soul.
我生命的最初部分是在乡村度过的，我们是农民，当时我们没有电力，用牲畜耕作土地，收集雨水，我们对大地怀有深深的敬意。希腊哲学家和诗人色诺芬说：“万物皆源于大地”，我们与自然和谐共处。我们收获的第一捆谷物，按照祖父的吩咐，送给了社区。从那时起，我学到了生命的伟大主题：利润与回馈之间的平衡；那段时光至今仍是我灵魂的馈赠。

As a boy, I saw my teary-eyed father, as he was subject to humiliation and offense at work, and even today I do not understand why he should be humiliated and belittled; however, inspired by the pain I read in those eyes, I decided that the dream of my life would be to live and work for the moral and economic dignity of the human being. I wanted a company that made healthy profits, but did so with ethics, dignity and morals; we are listed on the stock exchange, I wanted a company that had a balanced and gracious growth. I wanted human beings to work in slightly better places, earn a little more in wages and feel like thinking souls at work. Let us try not to turn our backs on poverty.
少年时，我看见我含泪的父亲在工作中遭受羞辱和冒犯，甚至到今天，我也不明白他为何要被羞辱和贬低；然而，从那些眼神中读到的痛苦激励了我，我决定我的人生梦想是生活和工作，为人类的道德和经济尊严。我想要一家能获得健康利润，但又能以道德、尊严和诚信经营的公司；我们在证券交易所上市，我想要一家公司能有平衡而优雅的成长。我想要人们能在稍好的地方工作，工资能稍微高一点，在工作中感觉像是有思想的灵魂。让我们试着不要背弃贫困。

I wanted a small part of the company's profits to go to beautify all of humanity and I wanted people to work a fair amount of hours and be online the right amount of time, so that Technology and Humanism could be harmonised and a healthy balance between mind, soul and body could be restored, because the soul and body also need nourishment every day.
我想要公司的一部分利润用来美化全人类，我想要人们工作公平的时长，在线时间也恰到好处，这样科技和人文就能和谐统一，心灵、灵魂和身体之间也能恢复健康的平衡，因为灵魂和身体也需要每天的滋养。

We try to respect the laws of every State, and thanks to my esteemed President Draghi, our Italy has regained its credibility.
我们努力尊重每个国家的法律，感谢我尊敬的总统德拉吉，我们的意大利已经重拾了信誉。

Our business is located in Solomeo, a small 14th century medieval village near Assisi. We work in old factories built in the past century, some have been restored and embellished to make them modern, others have been demolished and the land has been reclaimed for agriculture, especially vineyards, olive groves, orchards and wheat; so finally we can say that we have not consumed our beloved land. We have restored the village by listening to the wise word of our masters and we have built a theatre that we consider to be a secular temple of art, then a monument to the Dignity of Man and an immense park called "Project for Beauty".
我们的业务位于索洛梅奥，一个靠近阿西西的小型 14 世纪中世纪村庄。我们在过去一个世纪建造的老工厂里工作，一些工厂经过修复和装饰变得现代化，另一些则被拆除，土地被改造成农业用地，特别是葡萄园、橄榄园、果园和麦田；所以我们最终可以说我们没有消耗我们挚爱的土地。我们通过倾听我们导师的智慧之言来修复村庄，并建造了一个我们认为是世俗艺术之庙的剧院，然后是人类的尊严纪念碑，以及一个名为“美丽计划”的巨大公园。

And now we are going to build a universal library; for this idea we were inspired by the great Ptolemy I of Alexandria and the Emperor Hadrian when he stated: "Books showed me the way of life; when I grew up, life made me understand the content of books. Whoever builds libraries will have built public granaries for future generations".
现在我们要建造一座世界图书馆；这个想法让我们受到了亚历山大港伟大的托勒密一世和哈德良皇帝的启发，当他说：“书籍为我指明了生活的道路；当我长大时，生活让我理解了书籍的内容。谁建造图书馆，谁就为后代建造了公共粮仓”。

This is our idea of Human Sustainability and what we call Humanistic Capitalism. In greeting and thanking you, I hope my heart has suggested the right words for a request addressed to you, I like to believe, on behalf of the whole of mankind: "Oh my esteemed and powerful temporary guardians of Creation, you who are responsible for the beauty of the world, please show us the way to life. May Creation protect us and enlighten us towards a new universal Humanism".
这是我们对于人类可持续发展的理解，也是我们所说的“人文资本主义”。在致以问候和感谢之际，我希望我的心能为你传达恰当的话语，我愿意相信，这是代表全人类发出的请求：“哦，我尊贵而强大的临时地球守护者，你们负责世界的美丽，请为我们指明生存之路。愿地球保护我们，启迪我们走向一种新的世界人文主义”。

Ten Rules  十条规则



I. Greed does not benefit humans because it forces the rules of nature.
一、贪婪不会造福人类，因为它违背了自然法则。

II. Arrogance is not profitable, as it does not last long.
II. 傲慢无益，因其不能长久。

III. We act based on the belief that we are eternal: and so we build solidly and earnestly preserve what we have built.
III. 我们基于永恒存在的信念行事：因此我们坚实构建，并认真守护我们所构建的一切。

IV. We cultivate talent because its fruits nourish creativity.
IV. 我们培养人才，因为其成果滋养创造力。

V. The ambition to train and educate is the only one that counts beyond individual limits, because every decision must be made together, not by oneself.
V. 训练和教育之志是唯一超越个人局限的志向，因为每项决策都必须共同做出，而非独自一人。

VI. An international enterprise belongs to owners at an international level.
VI. 一家国际企业属于国际层面的所有者。

VII. The CEO of a company must be replaced every 12-15 years.
VII. 一家公司的 CEO 必须每 12-15 年更换一次。

VIII. If we are able to change, we will reinvent ourselves, just as nature reinvents itself.
第八章 如果我们能够改变，我们将重塑自我，正如自然重塑自我。

IX. For a company, sufficient liquidity is like mother yeast stored in a cupboard.
IX. 对于一家公司来说，充足的流动性就像橱柜里储存的酵母母亲。

X. Every ten years, more or less, there is a crisis, and after every crisis there is new growth: this is what history teaches us.
X. 每十年左右，总会有一场危机，而每次危机之后都会有新的成长：这就是历史教给我们的。

Brunello Cucinelli and Humanistic Capitalism
As for my business plans, I have three-year business plans and 30-year business plans but also three-centuries business plans. I think that this is another good breakthrough in the world. - Brunello Cucinelli as told to Om Malik in 2015.
关于我的商业计划，我有三年商业计划、三十年商业计划，还有三百年商业计划。我认为这是世界上另一个好的突破。-Brunello Cucinelli 于 2015 年告诉 Om Malik。

Brunello Cucinelli, 2021  Brunello Cucinelli，2021
Former farmer, current philosopher, and forever inspirational capitalist; Brunello Cucinelli is the founder of the Italian fashion powerhouse that bears his name. Dubbed the "king of cashmere" by The New Yorker, his company produces 100% made in Italy luxury items. But it's how he goes about running his company and living his life that is the most interesting.
前农民，现任哲学家，永远的激励型资本家；Brunello Cucinelli 是创立了以他名字命名的意大利时尚帝国的创始人。纽约客杂志称他为“羊绒之王”，他的公司生产 100%意大利制造的奢侈品。但最有趣的是他经营公司和生活的方式。

Om Malik calls him the "anti-LMVH". After watching House of Gucci I'd add he's the "anti-Gucci" as well.
Om Malik 称他为“反 LVHM”。在看了《古驰家族》后，我还会称他为“反古驰”。

He gives 20 percent of his company’s profits to his charitable foundation in the name of “human dignity” and pays his workers wages that are 20 percent higher than the industry standard, mostly because it allows his company to encourage and continue the Italian craftsman traditions. Cucinelli also pays for an artisan’s school in Solemeo: Young people are free to work either at his company or for another Italian company. The on-campus cafe is way more beautiful than Google Cafe or Facebook’s facilities. And the pasta is really heavenly.
他将公司利润的 20%捐赠给以“人类尊严”为名的慈善基金会，并支付给员工比行业标准高 20%的工资，这主要是因为这使他的公司能够鼓励和继续意大利工匠传统。库奇内利还在索莱梅奥资助了一所手工艺学校：年轻人可以免费在他公司或另一家意大利公司工作。校园内的咖啡馆比谷歌咖啡馆或 Facebook 的设施要漂亮得多。而且意大利面真的非常美味。
Business is good. The company expects to cross 1 billion euros in annual revenue by 2024 and has experienced strong growth YTD 2022.
生意很好。公司预计到 2024 年将实现年营收 100 亿欧元，并且 2022 年至今已实现强劲增长。

What is humanistic capitalism?
什么是人文资本主义？
What I mean by humanistic capitalism is precisely this idea, this concept of “fairness”. A fair and sustainable profit, a profit harmonised with giving back, and here comes the first main theme: striking a balance between profit and giving back; donating to the world as guardians of creation, leaving to those coming after us not the very same world we found, but a more amiable one.
我所说的“人文资本主义”正是这个理念，这个“公平”的概念。公平且可持续的利润，与回馈社会相协调的利润，这就是第一个主要主题：在利润和回馈之间取得平衡；作为创造的守护者，向世界捐赠，留给后来者一个比我们发现的更宜人的世界。
Secondly: making profit with ethics, dignity and morals. I don't know if I have succeeded, but this was my goal: not harming mankind in the process, or as little as possible.
其次：以道德、尊严和诚信来获取利润。我不知道我是否成功，但这曾是我的目标：在这个过程中不伤害人类，或者尽可能少地伤害。

My depiction of Humanistic Capitalism
我对人文资本主义的描绘
Themes:   主题：

Quality not quantity: Don’t waste time. Be focused. Be at your best. Know your craft.
质量而非数量：不要浪费时间。保持专注。发挥你的最佳状态。精通你的技艺。
Balance: Work hard, then rest. Become a master at doing nothing, otium.
平衡：努力工作，然后休息。成为无所事事的大师，otium。
Success is not only profits. The purpose of business is to benefit humanity, starting with the community closest to its roots.
成功不仅仅在于利润。商业的目的是造福人类，从其根基最接近的社区开始。
Human dignity: Make your work mean something. How you work and who you do it for matters.
人的尊严：让你的工作有意义。你如何工作和为谁工作都很重要。
Dignity of the worker  工人的尊严
Central to Brunello's thesis of humanistic capitalism is the dignity of the worker. This is deeply personal to Brunello as growing up he saw his father mistreated and struggle to provide and maintain his dignity.
在 Brunello 的人文资本主义理论中，工人的尊严是核心。这对 Brunello 来说意义重大，因为他成长过程中看到父亲受到虐待，努力提供和保持自己的尊严。

Several structural things Brunello has enacted set the stage for a work culture that admirably sets up workers to be their best.
Brunello 实施的一些结构性措施为一种工作文化奠定了基础，这种文化出色地让员工发挥出最佳状态。

No-one responds to emails after 5:30pm. No email can be sent to more than 3 people at a time.
没有人会在下午 5:30 之后回复邮件。一次最多只能向 3 个人发送邮件。
I want to take a step backward. Who remembers the last email they sent yesterday? No one. Or the last text message. Emperor Hadrian used to say, “The daily business, the daily life, the daily chores, kills the human being.“ I’m not interested in daily chores. We have now swapped information for knowledge, which is not the same thing. I do not want to know. I’m not online. I don’t even have a computer.
我想退一步。谁还记得昨天最后发送的那封邮件？没有人。或者最后发的短信。哈德良皇帝曾说：“日常事务、日常生活、日常琐事会杀死一个人。”我对日常琐事不感兴趣。我们现在用信息交换知识，但这并不相同。我不想知道。我不上网。我甚至没有电脑。
Everyone has a key to the building, there's a inherent trust and centralized structure.
每个人都有建筑的钥匙，存在着固有的信任和集中化的结构。

If you have 1,000 people, you have 1,000 geniuses. They’re just different kinds of genius and a different degree of intensity.
如果你有 1000 个人，你就有 1000 个天才。他们只是不同类型的天才，强度程度也不同。
They invest in maintaining beauty around their offices and factories. Renovating old buildings into modern workspaces.  
他们在维护其办公室和工厂周围的美丽方面进行投资。将旧建筑翻新为现代化的工作空间。

They provide free, gourmet food and wine with 90-minute lunches.
他们提供免费、美味的食物和葡萄酒，并提供 90 分钟的午餐。

But these perks carry with it a responsibility and expectation: bring your best.
但这些特权伴随着责任和期望：拿出你的最佳状态。

You must know all of your business with a 1 to 2 percent error rate. It is also training for your mind.
你必须了解你所有的业务，误差率在 1%到 2%之间。这也是对你思维的训练。

Investing in community and beautiful architecture
投资社区和美丽的建筑
In Brunello's mind, every business is a fabric of the community. Every business should serve and give back to the community that supports it just as the business is served by the people who live in the community and work there. It's a symbiotic relationship.
在 Brunello 的心中，每家企业都是社区的一张网。每家企业都应该服务于它所依赖的社区，并回馈社区，正如企业依赖于生活在社区中并为其工作的人们一样。这是一种共生关系。


Solomeo, Umbria, Italy  索洛梅奥，翁布里亚，意大利
The cities are hell, and we have to go back to living in small towns, and start debating again, and rethink humanity. - Rousseau
城市是地狱，我们必须回到小镇生活，重新开始辩论，重新思考人性。- 卢梭
Brunello has utilized profits and continuously reinvests them in the village of Solomeo in a big way: renovating buildings (or what Brunello calls "The Project for Beauty", developing a theatre, a library, and he even supports an artisan school where people can learn the craftsmanship of cashmere and the other items in the Brunello Cucinelli boutiques.
Brunello 利用利润，并且大力度地将它们持续再投资于索洛梅奥村：翻新建筑（或者说是 Brunello 称之为“美丽计划”），发展剧院、图书馆，他甚至支持一所手工艺学校，在那里人们可以学习 кашемир和其他 Brunello Cucinelli 精品店里的物品的工艺。


Front elevation of the Universal Library of Solomeo, drawing on paper.
Solomeo 图书馆的正立面，纸本绘制。
This vertical and horizontal integration harkens back to the symbiotic nature of Humanistic Capitalism. A staunch belief in the importance of a beautiful and quality working environment. An unwavering investment into the future: the people who work for him, and even those artisans who they train in the school that may go and work for another fashion house or pursue their own entrepreneurial dreams.
这种垂直和水平整合回溯到人文资本主义的共生本质。坚定地相信美丽且高质量的工作环境的重要性。坚定不移地投资未来：为他工作的人，甚至那些在学校接受培训的工匠，他们可能会去另一家时装公司工作，或者追求自己的创业梦想。

The human capital they're growing and developing is akin to the European style football clubs who have vibrant youth academies that sharpen the skills of young men and women. Many times they graduate to the 1st team of their beloved club (in this case it would be Brunello's company) but often they will get recruited or lured away to other clubs (a la Armani, Zegna, Gucci, etc.).
他们培养和发展的人力资本类似于欧洲风格的足球俱乐部，这些俱乐部拥有充满活力的青年学院，磨练年轻人的技能。他们经常毕业进入自己喜爱的俱乐部的第一队（在这种情况下，它将是 Brunello 的公司），但他们通常会被其他俱乐部招募或诱骗离开（比如 Armani、Zegna、Gucci 等）。


8 Insights to Brunello Cucinelli's Recipe of Business + Life Success
8 个关于 Brunello Cucinelli 商业与人生成功的启示
Invest in your people... From the workplace environment to the structure of the workday, to how you interact and collaborate to get work done. Dignity is a big word. Give it, live it.
投资你的员工……从工作场所环境到工作日的结构，再到你如何互动和协作来完成工作。尊严是一个大词。给予它，践行它。
Believe in humanity... What does"We love and respect each other" mean in practice? One way for BC: everyone has a key to the factory. Now from an operational security that sounds like a "er, no thanks". I've found in building high performing teams that arousing enthusiasm, showing appreciation and encouragement are key elements to empowering and encouraging everyone that their contribution matters. We all want to be a meaningful contributor as part of a winning team.
相信人性……"我们彼此相爱和尊重"在实践中意味着什么？对 BC 来说，有一种方式：每个人都有工厂的钥匙。现在从运营安全的角度来看，这听起来像是"呃，谢谢没兴趣"。我在建立高绩效团队时发现，激发热情、表达赞赏和鼓励是赋权和鼓励每个人认为他们的贡献很重要的关键要素。我们都希望成为获胜团队中一个有意义的贡献者。
Create a structure where 1 and 2 are lived out. Be consistent. If you do not live out the ideals for life and work then it's empty words. Refuse to check email after 5:30pm and do not come into the office on the weekends.
创建一个能够体现 1 和 2 的结构。要保持一致性。如果你在生活中和工作中的理想没有践行，那只是空话。拒绝在下午 5:30 后查看邮件，并在周末不去办公室。
Have the courage of your conviction. Be yourself... it will make you stand out from peers. Personality is profitable. Giving and philosophy and humanity is part of the brand, and you can't go back either (see #3). As Brunello told Om Malik: "I wanted the brand to have my face. I wanted the product to convey the culture, life, lifestyle, dignity of work."
要有你信念的勇气。做你自己……这将让你在同伴中脱颖而出。个性是有利可图的。给予、哲学和人性是品牌的一部分，你也不能回头（参见#3）。正如 Brunello 对 Om Malik 所说：“我希望品牌要有我的面孔。我希望产品能传达文化、生活、生活方式、工作的尊严。”
Go all in... Know what's non-negotiable. BC's 100% made in Italy and vertical integration has helped spur them into a record financial year in 2022.
